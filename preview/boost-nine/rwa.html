Evidence limitation — not proof of asset backing or legal rights

Validated XRPL ledger fields are not independent verification of real-world assets. The legal issuer identity, beneficial ownership, reserves or collateral backing, redemption rights, regulatory classification, liquidity and enforceability have NOT been independently verified.
